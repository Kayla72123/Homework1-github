Rom Coms

Romantic comedies, also known as Rom-Coms, are movies with the perfect balance of funny unseriousness and peak romance. I think modern-day Rom-Coms try to appeal to the "everywoman" by having a main character that experiences unconventional embarrassing moments to erase the stereotype that women are graceful and effortless.

27 Dresses

This movie is about a woman named Jane who is "always-a-bridesmaid" but never a bride. She meets a guy named Kevin after passing out at a wedding, and they have the classic enemies to lovers arc. I love this movie because of their dynamic. It has the perfect balance of comedy and romance. And most importantly, the lead male isn't a complete narcissistic jerk and actually acknowledges how hard-working she is (can you tell this was written by a woman). I know it's not the greatest rom-com, but I love it!
